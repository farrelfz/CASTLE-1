Keynote Speakers and Session Focus

Keynote profiles, research focus, and session format for CASTLE 6.0.

Dr. Sastia Prama Putri

Osaka University, Japan

Focused on data-driven metabolomics, biotechnology, and STEM or STEAM integration in science education.

  • Topic: Data-driven metabolomics and biotechnology.
  • Format: Keynote and Q and A session.

Prof. Rekha Koul

Curtin University, Australia

Highlights STEM education research, learning design, and practice-based curriculum development.

  • Topic: STEM education research and learning design.
  • Format: Keynote followed by discussion.

Wong Chee Hoo, Ph.D

INTI International University, Malaysia

Discusses innovation and collaboration across industry, education, and business through partnership practices.

  • Topic: Cross-sector innovation and collaboration.
  • Format: Keynote and Q and A session.

Participation Terms
